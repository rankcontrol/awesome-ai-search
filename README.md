## Data and statistics

- [G2 AI search insight report](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report) - G2 survey of software buyers: 51% start research with an AI chatbot more often than Google; 71% use one somewhere, as of March 2026.
- [TrustRadius, Bridging the Trust Gap](https://www.trustradius.com/blog/bridging-the-trust-gap-b2b-tech-buying-in-the-age-of-ai) - In 2024, 68% of surveyed buyers said generative AI had no impact on how they bought and 20% found it helpful, as of April 7, 2025.
- [TrustRadius, Beyond the Hype](https://www.trustradius.com/blog/beyond-the-hype) - Survey of 1,862 buyers in January 2026: 63% used AI during a purchase, and 94% of those users fact-checked it, as of July 15, 2026.
- [Forrester, B2B Buyers Make Zero-Click Number One](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/b2b_buyers_make_zero_click_buying_number_one/) - Analyst post drawing on Forrester's 2025 Buyers' Journey Survey: 94% of business buyers use AI in buying, up five points, as of January 22, 2026.
- [Forrester, 2026 B2B marketing, sales and product predictions](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-b2b-marketing-sales-product-2026-predictions) - Predicts at least one in five B2B sellers will answer AI-powered buyer agents with counteroffers during 2026, as of October 28, 2025.

- [Pew Research, Americans report using ChatGPT far more than other chatbots](https://www.pewresearch.org/chart/americans-report-using-chatgpt-far-more-than-other-chatbots/) - Share of US adults who have used each chatbot: ChatGPT 44%, Gemini 24%, Grok 8%, Claude 6%, as of February 2026.
- [Statcounter AI chatbot market share](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share) - Monthly chatbot share counted from visits sent to sites in Statcounter's network; worldwide, ChatGPT 51.58% and Gemini 28.86%, as of October 2026.

Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode (English) in 12 countries. No date is showing on this page. Checked on November 21, 2026.

- [Search Engine Journal, Why Reddit's ChatGPT Citation Drop Isn't Fully Explained](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/why-reddits-chatgpt-citation-drop-isnt-fully-explained/586479/) - Reports Promptwatch's figures. The average for Reddit was 3.83% of ChatGPT Search citations July 18 to August 7 vs. 0.52% August 14 to August 17, an 86.4% relative decrease. Promptwatch calls it provisional (as of August 19, 2026).
- [Promptwatch, Reddit citations are dropping in ChatGPT](https://promptwatch.com/data/reddit-citations-are-dropping-in-chatgpt) - ChatGPT daily citation share from July 7 to August 17, 2026, (with the decline starting Aug 8) while during this period Reddit's share declined 11.3% in Google AI Overviews & 30.5% in AI Mode (as of Aug 18, 2026)
- [Promptwatch, Social media citations by AI model, November 2026](https://promptwatch.com/data/social-media-citations-by-ai-model-november-2026) - Reddit's share of citations for the 30 days ending November 9th: 0.40% (October report 0.09%) for ChatGPT, 0.82% for Perplexity and 2.1% for Google AI Overviews. (as of Nov 10th, 2026)
- [Peec AI, GPT-5.6 retrieval changes](https://docs.peec.ai/research/gpt-56-retrieval-changes) - August 2026. Decreased to 43.5% from 94.0% in answers with one background search; increased from ~5% to 33.5% in second rounds; increased from ~12 to ~24 sources fetched per prompt.
- [Peec AI, GPT-5.6 retrieves product pages over listicles](https://docs.peec.ai/research/gpt-56-product-pages-vs-listicles) - Listicle pages dropped from 15.47% to 8.19% of retrieved pages. Product pages are at the top with 16.39%. Daily retrievals of product pages are up 119%. Page is dated August 2026.
- [Lily Ray, What We Can Learn from Evolving ChatGPT Fan-Out Queries](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/what-we-can-learn-from-evolving-chatgpt) - Aggregates research on ChatGPT site: searches like Nectiv's average of 2.17 to 7.61 fan-out queries per prompt, using site: in 64% of queries, plus one site: query example of ~197,000 impressions and 1 click as of August 17, 2026.
- [Netcraft, Large language models are falling for phishing scams](https://www.netcraft.com/blog/large-language-models-are-falling-for-phishing-scams) - 34% of 131 hostnames (for login pages of 50 brands) suggested by a GPT-4.1 family model were not owned by brands as of July 1, 2025.
