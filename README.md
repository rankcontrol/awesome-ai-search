## Data and statistics

- [G2 AI search insight report](https://learn.g2.com/g2-2026-ai-search-insight-report) - G2 survey of software buyers: 51% start research with an AI chatbot more often than Google; 71% use one somewhere, as of March 2026.
- [TrustRadius, Bridging the Trust Gap](https://www.trustradius.com/blog/bridging-the-trust-gap-b2b-tech-buying-in-the-age-of-ai) - In 2024, 68% of surveyed buyers said generative AI had no impact on how they bought and 20% found it helpful, as of April 7, 2025.
- [TrustRadius, Beyond the Hype](https://www.trustradius.com/blog/beyond-the-hype) - Survey of 1,862 buyers in January 2026: 63% used AI during a purchase, and 94% of those users fact-checked it, as of July 15, 2026.
- [Forrester, B2B Buyers Make Zero-Click Number One](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/b2b_buyers_make_zero_click_buying_number_one/) - Analyst post drawing on Forrester's 2025 Buyers' Journey Survey: 94% of business buyers use AI in buying, up five points, as of January 22, 2026.
- [Forrester, 2026 B2B marketing, sales and product predictions](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-b2b-marketing-sales-product-2026-predictions) - Predicts at least one in five B2B sellers will answer AI-powered buyer agents with counteroffers during 2026, as of October 28, 2025.
  Our write-up of the entries above: [How B2B Buyers Really Use ChatGPT to Pick Software (2026 Data)](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/how-b2b-buyers-really-use-chatgpt-to-pick-software-2026-data-af4bdc8098be).
- [Pew Research, Americans report using ChatGPT far more than other chatbots](https://www.pewresearch.org/chart/americans-report-using-chatgpt-far-more-than-other-chatbots/) - Share of US adults who have used each chatbot: ChatGPT 44%, Gemini 24%, Grok 8%, Claude 6%, as of February 2026.
- [Statcounter AI chatbot market share](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share) - Monthly chatbot share counted from visits sent to sites in Statcounter's network; worldwide, ChatGPT 51.58% and Gemini 28.86%, as of October 2026.
- [Similarweb, AI Search Stats in 2026](https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/) - Generative AI traffic analysis: 9.5 billion monthly web visits between June 2025 and May 2026, up 70%, as of September 17, 2026.
- [OpenRouter rankings](https://openrouter.ai/rankings) - Models and apps ordered by tokens processed through OpenRouter's API; the data is licensed CC BY 4.0, as of November 9, 2026.
- [Chrome Web Store, Grammarly extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/grammarly-ai-writing-assi/kbfnbcaeplbcioakkpcpgfkobkghlhen) - Store listing with 40,000,000 users and a 3.9 rating from 43.2K ratings, as of November 11, 2026.
- [Chrome Web Store, ChatGPT search extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/chatgpt-search/ejcfepkfckglbgocfkanmcdngdijcgld) - OpenAI's extension that makes ChatGPT the default search engine: 4,000,000 users, 3.3 rating from 1.6K ratings, as of November 11, 2026.
  Our write-up of the entries above: [The Most Popular AI Tools in 2026, Ranked Four Different Ways](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/the-most-popular-ai-tools-in-2026-ranked-four-different-ways-bab8287ca7f0).

## Comparisons

- [Google Search Central, AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) - Says AI Overviews and AI Mode may use query fan-out, and that no extra requirements apply beyond standard SEO, as of December 10, 2025.
- [Google Search Central Blog, Search Generative AI performance reports](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports) - Announces Search Console reports for AI Overviews and AI Mode, listing impressions, pages, countries, devices and dates, as of June 3, 2026.
- [Bing Webmaster Blog, AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/) - Public preview of a report that counts total citations of a site in AI-generated answers, as of February 10, 2026.
  Our write-up of the entries above: [AI Search vs Google Search: Why 1% of Traffic Still Shapes Shortlists](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/ai-search-vs-google-search-why-1-of-traffic-still-shapes-shortlists-c630193343d6).
- [Perplexity Help Center, How does Perplexity work?](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352895-how-does-perplexity-work) - States that each answer carries numbered citations linking to the original sources; the page shows no date, checked November 11, 2026.
  Our write-up: [AI SEO Agency vs AEO Software: $400 a Month or Five Figures?](https://medium.com/@thesearchshift/ai-seo-agency-vs-aeo-software-400-a-month-or-five-figures-a48a33680491).
